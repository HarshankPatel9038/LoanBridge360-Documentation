# 🌉 LoanBridge360 — User Guide

**LoanBridge360** is a free web app to track loans and interest — money you **gave** to someone or **received** from someone (a bank or a person). It calculates interest automatically, lets both sides of a loan verify payments together, and keeps everything organized in one place.

### 🔗 Open the App
👉 **[https://loanbridge360.vercel.app/](https://loanbridge360.vercel.app/)**

> ⚠️ **Best experience: use the Desktop / Laptop version.**
> LoanBridge360 works on mobile too, but the dashboard, tables, and loan-sharing screens are designed for a **desktop browser**. For the smoothest experience, open the link above on a computer.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Go to **[https://loanbridge360.vercel.app/](https://loanbridge360.vercel.app/)**

## ✨ All Features Explained

### 💰 1. Loan Tracking
- Add a loan in either direction:
  - **🤝 I Received Money** — you borrowed, you owe someone.
  - **💸 I Gave Money** — you lent, someone owes you.
- Works for **Banks** or **Individuals/People**.
- Choose **Simple** or **Compound** interest.
- Interest rate can be **Yearly** or **Monthly**.
- The app **automatically calculates**: interest earned so far, total amount payable, amount already paid, and remaining balance — always up to date, no manual math needed.
- See an **upcoming schedule** — what you'll owe (or be owed) over the next 6 periods.
- Edit or delete any entry anytime.
- Once a loan is fully settled, **Mark it as Closed** — it moves to your Archive. You can **Reopen** it later if needed.

### 🤝 2. Shared Loans — Track Together With the Other Person
Instead of both people keeping separate notes (which never match!), you can connect a loan so both sides see the **same numbers**:
- Open a loan → click **Connect/Share** → a unique **Secure Room Code** is generated (e.g. `Bridge42!-Vault17@-Secure`).
- Share this code with the other person (WhatsApp, email, etc.).
- They open LoanBridge360, choose **Join a Loan**, paste the code, and request to join.
- You'll see their request and can **Allow** or **Reject** it.
- Once connected, you can control whether they're allowed to add entries, **remove** them anytime, or they can choose to **leave** the shared loan themselves.

### ✅ 3. Payment Verification (No Fake Entries)
- When you add a payment, it shows as **⏳ Pending** for the other connected person.
- They must **Verify ✅** it for it to count, or **Reject ❌** it if it's wrong.
- This means neither side can secretly change numbers — everything is agreed on by both people.

### 📊 4. Dashboard & Reports
- **Overview** — total given, total borrowed, total interest earned/owed, total repayments, outstanding balances — all in one glance.
- Filter by **All / Given / Received** loans.
- **Parties view** — see totals grouped by each person or bank.
- **Sandbox / Copies** — create a "what-if" test copy of a loan to try out different scenarios, without touching your real numbers.
- **Activity Log** — a timestamped history of everything: loans created, payments added, connections made, loans closed.
- Switch between **List view** and **Grid view** for browsing your loans.

### 🌍 5. Multi-Currency Display
- Change the currency you view your amounts in (INR, USD, EUR, GBP, and more).
- Exchange rates update automatically — this only changes how numbers are *displayed*, your actual saved data stays consistent.

### 👤 6. Your Profile & Account
- Update your **name** and **profile photo**.
- **Forgot your password?** Use the reset-password link on the sign-in screen — a reset email will be sent to you.
- **Delete your account** anytime from Settings — it's not deleted instantly. You get a **30-day grace period**, and if you log back in during that time, the deletion is automatically cancelled.

### 🎨 7. Look & Feel
- Toggle **Dark Mode / Light Mode**.
- Everything updates **in real time** — if you or your connected partner makes a change on another device, you'll see it instantly without refreshing.

## 🧭 Step-by-Step: How to Use Everything

### ➕ Adding a Loan
1. Click **Add New Entry**.
2. Pick the direction (Received / Gave).
3. Pick Bank or Person, and enter their name.
4. Enter the amount, interest rate, and whether it's yearly/monthly.
5. Pick the start date.
6. Choose Simple or Compound interest.
7. Click **Save Entry**. Done — the dashboard updates automatically.

### 💵 Adding a Payment
1. Open the loan.
2. Click to add a payment and enter the amount.
3. If it's a shared loan, wait for the other person to **Verify** it.

### 🔗 Sharing a Loan
1. Open the loan → **Connect/Share**.
2. Copy the **Secure Room Code** shown and send it to the other person.
3. They paste it under **Join a Loan** and request access.
4. You approve/reject their request from the same screen.

### 📁 Closing a Loan
1. Open the fully-paid loan.
2. Click **Mark as Closed**.
3. Find it later under **Closed Loans** — reopen anytime if needed.

### ⚙️ Changing Settings
- **Currency / Theme**: available from the sidebar or settings menu.
- **Name / Photo**: Settings → edit profile.
- **Password**: Sign-in screen → "Forgot password?" or from Settings.

### 🗑️ Deleting Your Account
1. Settings → **Delete Account**.
2. Read the warning, click **I Understand, Continue**.
3. Enter your password to confirm.
4. Your account is scheduled for deletion in 30 days. Log in anytime before then to cancel it automatically.

## 🚨 Important Things to Know

- **🖥️ Use the Desktop version for the best experience.** The site works on phones, but is built and tested primarily for desktop browsers — for full functionality and the best look, use a laptop or computer.

- **📧 Use a real, valid email — don't try fake/temporary emails.**
  - Sign-up only accepts genuine email providers (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook, iCloud, etc.).
  - If you try to sign up with a **fake, invalid, or disposable/temporary email**, you will be **blocked** and shown a message like:
    > "Temporary or disposable email addresses are not allowed. Please use a real email address."
  - If this happens, don't keep retrying with fake emails — **wait some time and try again later** using a real, working email address that you can actually check (a confirmation link is sent to it).

- **A payment isn't final until the other person verifies it** on a shared loan — this protects both sides from wrong entries.

- **Deleting your account is not instant** — you have a 30-day safety window. Logging back in during that time cancels the deletion automatically and keeps all your data safe.

## ❓ FAQ / Troubleshooting

**Q: I signed up but didn't get a confirmation email.**
A: Check your spam/junk folder. Make sure you used a real, supported email provider.

**Q: It says my email isn't allowed / I'm blocked from signing up.**
A: You used a temporary/disposable or unsupported email domain. Use a real Gmail/Yahoo/Outlook/iCloud etc. address. If you're still blocked, wait a while and try again with a valid email.

**Q: Why is my payment still showing "Pending"?**
A: It needs to be **verified by the other connected person** on that shared loan. Ask them to open the loan and verify it.

**Q: I want to undo my account deletion.**
A: Just **log back in** before the 30 days are up — it cancels automatically.

**Q: The app looks cramped or broken on my phone.**
A: LoanBridge360 is optimized for **desktop**. Try opening [loanbridge360.vercel.app](https://loanbridge360.vercel.app/) on a computer for the best experience.
